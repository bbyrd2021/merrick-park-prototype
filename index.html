Our story
began in 1928.

Merrick Park Baptist Church was incorporated in 1928 with seven adults and two children in St. Albans, Queens.

Bishop Curtis G. Norton, Sr. assumed leadership in 1960. During his tenure, the congregation moved to its home at 120-02 Marsden Street in Jamaica.
